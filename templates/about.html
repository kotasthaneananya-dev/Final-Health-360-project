About This Project

Smart Healthcare Analytics: Patient Risk & Lifestyle Insights is a comprehensive data-driven platform built to analyze correlations between patient lifestyle choices, medical risk factors, demographics, and chronic disease prevalence.

Using interactive Tableau visualizations and robust Python Flask web routing, this platform offers clear analytical dashboards covering variables such as Body Mass Index (BMI) categories, exercise habits, blood pressure distribution across age groups, and predictive health indicators.
